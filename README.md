# Road Energy Harvesting for Smart & Sustainable Roads

A smart road energy-harvesting system that captures mechanical energy generated by moving vehicles and converts it into usable electrical energy.

## Overview

The system uses a pressure plate installed beneath selected road sections. When a vehicle passes over the plate, its load creates mechanical movement that is transferred through a rack-and-pinion and gear mechanism to a generator.

**Vehicle Load → Pressure Plate → Rack & Pinion → Gear Transmission → Generator → Power Conditioning → Energy Storage**

## Key Features

* Vehicle-based mechanical energy harvesting
* Pressure plate and mechanical transmission system
* Rack-and-pinion mechanism for motion conversion
* Generator-based electrical energy generation
* Voltage conditioning and energy storage
* Monitoring of generated energy and system status
* Potential support for low-power road infrastructure

## Applications

The harvested energy can support low-power applications such as:

* Road safety indicators
* Traffic sensors
* Street-light support
* IoT monitoring devices

## Prototype

The prototype demonstrates vehicle movement, pressure-plate activation, mechanical energy conversion, voltage generation, and energy storage through a simulated smart-road environment.

## Technology

**Hardware:** Pressure plate, springs, rack and pinion, gears, generator, rectifier, voltage regulator, battery/supercapacitor, microcontroller and sensors.

**Software:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript and real-time monitoring dashboard.

## Future Scope

The system can be further developed using real-world traffic testing, improved mechanical efficiency, IoT monitoring, modular road installations, and optimization for high-traffic locations.
