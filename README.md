# Smart Road Energy Harvesting System

A smart and sustainable road-energy harvesting system that converts mechanical energy generated by moving vehicles into usable electrical energy.

The system uses vehicle pressure on a road-mounted mechanism to capture mechanical energy and convert it into electrical energy. The generated energy can be stored and monitored for use in low-power road infrastructure.

**Working:**
Vehicle Movement → Pressure Plate → Mechanical Energy Conversion → Electrical Energy → Energy Storage → Monitoring

## Key Features

* Vehicle movement simulation
* Pressure plate activation
* Mechanical energy harvesting
* Real-time voltage monitoring
* Energy storage monitoring
* Pressure event tracking
* System health and diagnostics
* Interactive monitoring dashboard

## Applications

* Road safety indicators
* Traffic sensors
* IoT monitoring devices
* Smart road infrastructure
* Low-power lighting systems

## Technologies

* HTML
* CSS
* JavaScript

## Future Scope

The system can be extended with a physical prototype, real traffic testing, IoT connectivity, improved energy conversion efficiency, and deployment at high-traffic locations.
